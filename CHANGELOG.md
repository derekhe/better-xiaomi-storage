# Changelog

## 0.2.0-alpha.1 - 2026-10-12

- Add an integrated Xiaomi account login and storage-device selection flow.
- Manage an independent device tunnel without running the full official client.
- Add account sign-out and isolated login profiles.
- Experimental preview: real account login and device access still require validation.

---

- 添加小米账号登录和存储设备选择流程。
- 独立管理设备连接通道，无需运行完整的官方客户端。
- 支持退出账号，并使用独立的登录数据目录。
- 实验预览版：真实账号登录和设备读取仍待验证。

## 0.1.1 - 2026-09-01

- Open photos in a full-screen, fit-to-window original image viewer.
- Play videos with native controls and range-based seeking.
- Show clear video badges in the gallery and fall back to a large preview for unsupported image formats.

---

- 点击照片后以全屏、适应窗口的方式查看原图。
- 使用原生播放器播放视频，并支持基于字节范围的进度跳转。
- 在相册中显示视频标识；不受支持的图片格式自动回退到大尺寸预览图。

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-01

- Browse Xiaomi Smart Storage photos in a fast, read-only desktop gallery.
- Jump directly to a year, month, or day in large photo libraries.
- Navigate albums, search, filter media types, and preview photos.
- Load a lightweight timeline index first and thumbnails on demand.

---

- 通过快速、只读的桌面相册浏览小米智能存储中的照片。
- 在大型照片库中按年、月、日快速定位。
- 支持相册导航、搜索、媒体类型筛选和照片预览。
- 优先加载轻量时间索引，并按需加载缩略图。
